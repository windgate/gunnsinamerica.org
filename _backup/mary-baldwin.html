Colonial Era · 1635

Jasper Gunn Arrives in New England

A young man leaves England for an uncertain shore — what brought him, what he found, and how he put down roots that would hold for centuries.

Era Colonial
Date Range 1635 – 1660
Key Figure Jasper Gunn
Location Massachusetts Bay Colony

In the spring of 1635, a young man named Jasper Gunn boarded a ship in England and set sail for the Massachusetts Bay Colony. He was among the thousands who crossed the Atlantic during the Great Migration — that decade-long flood of Puritan settlers who would lay the foundations of New England society. What drove him, precisely, we cannot say with certainty. What we know is that he arrived, that he stayed, and that his descendants would carry the Gunn name across American history for nearly four centuries.

The World He Left Behind

England in the 1630s was a country under pressure. Archbishop Laud's campaign to enforce Anglican conformity had made life uncomfortable — and sometimes dangerous — for those with Puritan sympathies. Add to that the economic strain of the period, the lure of land in the New World, and the pull of a community already forming across the ocean, and the decision to emigrate becomes easier to understand, if no less momentous.
