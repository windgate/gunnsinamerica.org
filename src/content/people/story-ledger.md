# Christian, Mary, Anne: reconciliation ledger

The Christian Gunn article is written to the evidence below. Each row lists a conflict across the site, what the records support, and what to change.

## The substantiated story

| Tier | Statement | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documented | Jasper Gun married Anne Legge at Sandon, Essex, 14 Jan 1633 | Sandon parish register (reference to add) |
| Documented | Jasper sailed on the *Defence* in 1635 with a woman recorded as Ann or Anne, surname Goun | Hotten's List (spelling and ages to check) |
| Working identification | That woman is Anne Legge of Sandon, Jasper's first known wife; the 1607 Great Burstead baptism, the 1633 marriage, and the 1635 passenger are one man | Name, county, date, and ages agree; no other Jasper Gun/Gunn found in Essex and neighboring counties (search scope to record) |
| Assumption | Anne died during the voyage or soon after | No record of her after 1635; no death record found |
| Reported | Jasper and wife Mary joined the Milford church 25 Apr 1641, dismissed from Hartford | Anderson, vol. III (wording to check) |
| Documented | Jasper's will, 25 Dec 1670, calls his wife "their mother" and does not name her | Probate vol. 1, pt. 2, pp. 39–40 (reading edition; original not yet examined) |
| Documented | Christian Gunn, widow of Jasper, made her will 6 May 1690; proved 1 Dec 1690 | New Haven probate; site pages |
| Reported | Christian's will names the same children as Jasper's | Abbott, 316–24 (will text not yet seen) |
| Conjecture | Christian was Christian Baldwin of Cholesbury, bapt. 1615/16 | Circumstances only: a shared first name, a workable age, and nearby Baldwin relatives. No record names Jasper's wife as a Baldwin. Not to be used as her maiden name |
| Unproved | Christian bore Jobamah, Nathaniel, Mehitable, or Abel | No record examined |
| Unproved | Mary and Christian are one woman | No record examined |
