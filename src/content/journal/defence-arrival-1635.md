The *Defence of London*, master Edward Bostock, sailed from London on 14 July 1635
and arrived at Boston harbor on 8 October — six weeks after the Great Colonial Hurricane
had devastated New England. On the passenger roll, the Custom House clerk recorded them
as *Jasper Gonn*, aged 29, and *Ann Gonn*, aged 25.

One detail worth flagging: sources consistently put the arrival date at October 8, not
October 7. Worth verifying against the original Custom House record at the UK National
Archives (State Papers Domestic, SP 16) — the enrollment date of July 18 is separately
confirmed. The original manuscript, if digitized, would make a striking image for the article.
