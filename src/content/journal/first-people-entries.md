Jasper and Christian Gunn are now the first two biographies on the site, each
linked to the other as spouses. Getting there meant settling a question that's
followed this line for generations: what was Jasper's wife actually named?

The documentary answer is Christian — the name in her own 1690 will. "Mary" and
the "Baldwin" maiden name are later attributions the records don't support, so
the site treats them as tradition rather than fact.

More entries to follow as the People section grows.
